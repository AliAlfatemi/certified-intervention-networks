## TopoHub / Abilene underlay

`cin/data/public_topologies/abilene.json` is a compact transformation of the
Abilene topology distributed by the TopoHub repository, which attributes the
underlay to the Internet Topology Zoo collection.

- Upstream project: Piotr Jurkiewicz, **TopoHub**
- Paper: *TopoHub: A repository of reference Gabriel graph and real-world
  topologies for networking research*, SoftwareX 24 (2023), 101540
- DOI: `10.1016/j.softx.2023.101540`
- Upstream record: `https://github.com/piotrjurkiewicz/topohub/blob/master/data/topozoo/Abilene.json`
- Upstream repository license: MIT

The vendored file retains only node identifiers, city labels, and undirected
links. The role, threat, service, telemetry, endpoint-attachment, and action-
cost overlays used by this artifact are newly generated synthetic data.
